# idem research

Research and infrastructure for people who delegate real work to agents and want to stay in control of it.

We work on one part of human–AI collaboration: delegation with control.

- What may an agent do without asking?
- When should it hand the work back?
- What does a person need in order to decide well?
- How do you change or revoke a permission without losing the work?

We answer with software you can run and experiments you can check.

## sats.sh

Our first product. An agent spends money on your behalf within limits you set, and hands the decision back the moment a payment goes over them. Money makes permissions, limits and consequences explicit, which makes it the right place to start.
